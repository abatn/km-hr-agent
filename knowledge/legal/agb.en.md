2.1 The provider operates a digital platform that structures the application process between

2.2 The provider acts **exclusively as a digital intermediary**. It is **not** an employer, **not**
a temporary employment agency and **not** a recruitment agency in the legal sense. **No**
employment relationship arises between the provider and a candidate. Hiring decisions, contracts,
salaries and visa/residence permit decisions lie solely with the employers and the competent
authorities.

2.3 There is **no guarantee** of placement, a training position or a visa.

2.4 Matching and scoring are produced with **AI assistance**; the final decision on invitation,
rejection and hiring is taken exclusively by humans. Details: Privacy Policy (`/datenschutz`),
